The pre-registered λ=0.05 retune FAILED again, all four readings. The λ=0.10 retune (last committed fallback) FAILED 3 of 4 readings; the one reading that passed (`lam010_retune_b`/coa_phase) turned out on scatter-plot inspection to be a **std_ratio false-positive** — the predicted-vs-true plot shows a flat, mode-collapsed constant output (two bands offset by 2π, no per-sample tracking), present from epoch 5 through epoch 80, not real learning that a summary statistic alone would have missed catching. **Verdict of record, per the preregistration's own decision table: "λ alone insufficient for this architecture/head, post-formula-fix."** This is a negative result about the retune mechanism, not the corrected-formula degeneracy hypothesis — the central question remains formally unanswered under this line of attack. See `NOTES.md`'s 2026-09-16 entries for full detail, including the scatter-plot correction.
