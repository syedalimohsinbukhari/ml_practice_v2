## Why this redo existed

The original investigation (`experiments/phic_psi_poc/`) tested whether a network can recover the individual coalescence phase (`φc`) and polarization angle (`ψ`) from strain, using the combination `φc±2ψ`.
A reviewer later found that combination physically wrong — the injection convention makes `φc` enter the waveform doubled too, so the correct combination is `2φc±2ψ`.
Full evidence chain for the fix: `experiments/phic_psi_poc/diagnostic_log.md`, dated entry 2026-09-09.
The original's null result (the network learns nothing about `φc`/`ψ`) was therefore **unestablished, not confirmed wrong** — it was possible the wrong formula, not a genuine physical degeneracy, explained the failure to learn.
This redo exists to answer that specific question under the corrected formula, and — per explicit instruction partway through — to redo every check the original ran, not only the ones expected to change.

## Central verdict: `coa_phase`/`polarization_angle` are not recoverable, on strong and convergent evidence

**Two separate lines of evidence both point the same direction, though they answer slightly different questions and carry different formal status.**

**1. The pre-registered, gated λ-retune path stayed formally UNINTERPRETABLE, not NULL.** `preregistration_lam_retune.md`'s Step 0 std_ratio gate — testing specifically whether `poc_redo_b`'s `combo_A`/`combo_B` vectors stabilize — never cleared, at any of the three λ values tried (0.01, 0.05, 0.10; `diagnostic_logvar_gate.py`'s results, `NOTES.md` 2026-09-15/16). λ=0.10 (the preregistration's last committed fallback) failed 3 of 4 readings; the one reading that passed turned out, on scatter-plot inspection, to be a std_ratio false-positive — a mode-collapsed constant output, not real learning. Verdict of record on this specific, narrow target: **"λ alone insufficient for this architecture/head, post-formula-fix."** Steps 1–3 of the preregistration (bootstrap significance, effect size, SNR stratification on the combo angles themselves) never ran, because they were gated on Step 0 passing.

**2. The broader, ungated Stage 2b(i) evidence (2026-09-18) answers a wider version of the same question directly, and converges cleanly on a real null.** Four independent methods, all against the certified 4-model set (`poc_a`, `poc_b`, `tcn`, `cnn_attention`):

- **`analyse_predictions.py`**: `poc_a`/`poc_b`'s `coa_phase`/`polarization_angle` predictions are genuinely mode-collapsed — near-delta-function output regardless of input (`poc_b`'s `polarization_angle`: 99.9% of 5,000 validation predictions in one histogram bin). `tcn`/`cnn_attention` are noisy/uninformative rather than collapsed, a different failure mode with the same conclusion.
- **`diagnostic_checks.py`** (Checks 2, 4, 6): loss wiring confirmed correct in both training modes (cross-checked against `trainer.py`'s actual source, not just its `head_loss` dict listing); the full gradient chain from `combo_A_pred`/`combo_B_pred` back through the corrected `tf_double_angle` stage to the raw model output is healthy end-to-end on the real trained checkpoint — **this rules out an implementation bug as the explanation for the collapse.**
- **`bootstrap_ang_mae.py`**: a formal, 10,000-iteration shuffle-null significance test finds `coa_phase`/`polarization_angle` statistically indistinguishable from chance (mostly *worse* than chance) in **every one of the 4 models** — the most direct, rigorous answer this redo produced to its own central question.
- **`inclination_stratification.py`/`inclination_control_stratification.py`/`snr_stratification.py`**: no model clears this investigation's own 0.10 rad materiality floor in any inclination band or SNR tercile, for either head.

**Bottom line: under the corrected `2φc±2ψ` formula, on this architecture set, `φc`/`ψ` information is not recoverable from strain — a well-evidenced null, not an artifact of the original's wrong-formula testing.** The gated λ-retune path's own formal language ("UNINTERPRETABLE") is accurate to its own narrow scope and is left on the record unedited, per this repo's frozen-vs-living-docs convention; it is superseded in practical confidence, not contradicted, by the broader evidence above. Reconciling these two into a single formal preregistered statement was flagged as an open decision earlier in this process and remains one — this summary states the evidence-weighted bottom line without rewriting either party's own recorded verdict.

## Secondary findings — real, and worth carrying forward on their own

**`cnn_attention` shows genuine, SNR-consistent partial sky-localization skill — the first result in this entire investigation (original or redo) to pass every check this repo's discipline requires of a positive finding.** `bootstrap_sky_separation.py` found all 4 models significantly better than random at `sky_position`; `poc_a`/`poc_b`/`tcn` are small-but-real and sub-floor (Δ≈2.9–3.4°, like `inclination` below), but `cnn_attention`'s effect (Δ=12.58°, z=+23.23σ) clears the 0.10 rad floor outright. A direct check ruled out the simplest population-bias explanation (always guessing the population-mean direction only scores ~89.4°, essentially at the null, not `cnn_attention`'s 77.5°). The decisive follow-up, `snr_stratification_sky_position.py`, resolved it cleanly: `cnn_attention`'s localization error shrinks monotonically across all 3 SNR terciles (80.15°→77.95°→74.47°), and the high-SNR tercile alone clears the floor by an even larger margin (Δ=+15.53°) — the signature of a real per-sample strain→direction mapping, not a memorization shortcut (which would have no reason to track signal loudness). The specific physical channel — most likely cross-detector H1/L1 amplitude-ratio information via sky-dependent antenna pattern, since this dataset's 2-detector setup can't do full timing triangulation — was never directly tested, only found consistent with the evidence. **Peripheral to the central φc/ψ question, unaffected either way**, but a genuine discovery of this redo, not noise.

**`cnn_attention` also memorizes, on a different pair of heads.** `plot_certified_memorization.py` found its `coa_phase`/`polarization_angle` training-split circular loss drops to ~0.55 while validation stays flat at ~1.0 (train–val gap ≈0.44–0.47, vs. 0.003–0.05 for the other three models) — invisible in every previously-reported number because the Round-1 summary only ever tracked validation loss. This is why the `sky_position` finding above needed the SNR-stratification check before being trusted: this exact model has a documented, unrelated tendency to overfit specific heads, making "is this real or memorized" a live question worth actually checking rather than assuming either way.

**`inclination` is a small, real, sub-floor signal in all 4 models — not the "pure noise floor" this investigation's stratification methodology assumed it was.** `bootstrap_ang_mae.py` found `inclination` significant (p≤0.022, all 4 models, up to p=0.0003 for `cnn_attention`) but with a small effect (~0.02–0.04 rad, below the 0.10 rad floor). Verified against the Round-1 scatter plots directly, not just the p-value — all four models show a genuine, if noisy, diagonal trend in the `ι` panel, visibly different from `φc`/`ψ`'s flat collapsed bands in the same images, ruling out a large-N statistical artifact with no real structure behind it. Any future argument that leans on `inclination` as a strict noise floor (as `inclination_stratification.py`'s own framing does) should carry this caveat.

**One undisclosed methodological property, present in both investigations, corrected here.** `poc_b`'s individual `coa_phase`/`polarization_angle` bootstrap numbers rest on ground-truth-assisted branch reconstruction (`formulae_reference.md` A.8) — true in the original's own `bootstrap_ang_mae.py` run too, disclosed in neither's original write-up. Traced and confirmed via direct grep of both investigations' full doc sets. Doesn't change either verdict (both read non-significant for `poc_b` on those two heads, and the bias this property could introduce runs toward false-positive significance, not false-negative), but it's now on the record.

## Redo-completeness: every original check has a counterpart

Per explicit instruction partway through this process, every `.py` check the original investigation ran was audited against this directory and, where missing, ported — not just the ones expected to change under the corrected formula. Full list and disposition: `experiment_index.md`'s "What's still open?" section and its Code table. Two corrections were needed during porting, not just repointing: `diagnostic_checks.py`'s Check 6 had hardcoded the pre-correction combo formula (fixed to include the `tf_double_angle` step, verified against a synthetic-tensor test); `plot_certified_memorization.py`'s "2 null-certified models" framing didn't apply to a redo that has certified no null, so it was adapted to all 4 down-select-confirmed models instead. The only original files with no redo counterpart are the per-λ training-launcher wrapper scripts, which the redo's own `config_*.yaml` + `train_poc.py` workflow already covers directly — not a gap.

A second-opinion review of this session's scatter-vs-p-value discipline added three more scripts (`rose_plot_residuals.py`, `sky_position_mollweide.py`, superseded by `sky_angular_separation_hist.py`) plus the `sky_position`-specific `bootstrap_sky_separation.py` and `snr_stratification_sky_position.py`. Most of that review's other points were already handled correctly (period conventions, `circular_r` as the existing headline statistic); one specific claim ("a vMF calibration issue you've already flagged as deferred") was checked and found to correspond to nothing in either investigation's actual record — not acted on.
